# EarthPress user requirements

What users need, written from their side. Product requirements that meet these needs are in EPR-PRS-001 (`03-requirements.md`) and the links are in the traceability matrix EPR-RTM-001 (`03b-traceability.md`). They are drawn from desk research and the earlier requirements; none has yet been confirmed with users, which is the first task with the co-design partner (EPR-PRB-001). Method: STANDARDS section 22.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Performance | Standard block size and height | Every block is the same size and height, so walls run true and mortar joints stay thin | High | Masons and builders (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Dense, strong blocks | Blocks are pressed hard enough to be strong and durable when the soil and mix are right | High | Self-builders; NGO trainers; architects (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-003 | Performance | Output for a house | A small crew makes enough blocks in a day to build a small house in about a week of pressing | High | Block-making enterprises; self-builders (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-004 | Usability | Ordinary adults can operate it | Ordinary adults, including first-time volunteers, can press blocks without straining, two people sharing the work if needed | High | Self-builders; NGO trainers (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-005 | Deployment and assembly | Built and repaired by a local welder | A local welder can build and repair the press from common steel with a stick welder, grinder and drill press | High | Local welders and fabricators (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-006 | Logistics | Movable between sites | The press can be moved between sites on a pickup and carried in pieces by two people | Medium | NGO trainers; self-builders (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-007 | Maintenance | Long life, replaceable wear parts | The press lasts many thousands of blocks, and the parts that wear can be replaced with hand tools | Medium | Block-making enterprises (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-008 | Usability | Mix chosen with field tests | Builders can tell whether a soil is suitable and how much stabilizer it needs, with simple field tests and no laboratory | High | Self-builders; NGO trainers (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-009 | Cost | Affordable press and kit | The press and soil kit cost a few hundred dollars in parts, far below hydraulic machines | High | Self-builders; NGOs; funders (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-010 | Environment and durability | Low stabilizer use | Blocks need little cement or lime, which is the main running cost | Medium | Self-builders; block-making enterprises (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-011 | Environment and durability | Lower carbon than fired brick | Walls made with the blocks carry much less embodied carbon than fired brick | Medium | Architects; funders (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-012 | Safety | Safe for operators and bystanders | Nobody is hurt by the lever, the lid, pinch points, cement or dust when the press is used as instructed | High | NGO trainers; funders; all users (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-013 | Regulatory | Open documentation | Drawings, parts list and build plan are open and complete enough to build without contacting the author | Medium | Local welders; NGOs (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-014 | Usability | Human powered | The press needs no fuel, electricity or hydraulics on site | High | Self-builders on rural sites (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-015 | Environment and durability | Works outdoors in dust and rain | The press keeps working outdoors in dust, heat and rain | Medium | Self-builders; block-making enterprises (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-016 | Regulatory | Blocks testable against a strength reference | Blocks can be tested against a recognized strength reference for the intended use | Medium | Architects; building authorities (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
